# The five stages of ethical hacking

## Reconnaissance
- This stage is also known as information gathering
- There is active and passive reconnaissance
- Passive
  - Going to google or social media to gather information
- Active
  - Using tools to scan actively against a client
  - We are looking for open ports and vulnerabilities

## Scanning and Enumeration
- When scanning we also perform enumeration
- Enumeration is gathering information by researching

## Gaining Access (Exploitation)
- Running an exploit against a client to try and gain access to a machine, network, or environment
- Once access is gained repeat the process

## Maintaining Access
- If we were to get kicked out how do we maintain access

## Covering Tracks
- Delete any logs, malware, accounts created, and just generally cleanup
